# UNINTENDED SHIFT TO THIRD PERSON PERSPECTIVE\[TPP\] WITH LOSS OF MOVEMENT CONTROL AND CLIP COLLISION DURING RANDOM ENCOUNTERS 

# SEVERITY

Medium

# PRIORITY

P2

# GAME TITLE/VERSION

THE WALKING ZOMBIE 2/ V3.59.0

# PLATFORM/DEVICE

Andriod/moto edge 70

# SUMMARY

After completing a mission at the train station inn sun city and
traveling back towards camp,triggering a random encounter (eg.vikings
encounter) causes the game camera to forcibly switch to third-person
perspective(TPP). the player loses directional control,weapons
functionality,and collision detection( clipping through
wall/NPC),leading to inevitable player death.

# STEPS TO REPRODUCE

1.  Launch The walking zombie 2 on Andriod.

2.  complete a mission at the train station location in sun city.

3.  Travel back towards the Base camp to trigger a travel encounter

4.  Enter the encounter (eg.viking encounter).

5.  Observer the camera perspective,collision state,and player controls
    upon spawning.

# EXPECTED RESULT

The player spawns in the encounter map in first person perspective(FPP)
with full movement control,active collision,and functional weapons to
defend against incoming enemies.

# ACTUAL RESULT

Te camera forcibly shifft to (TPP). the player suffer complete loss of
directional movement,weapons handling, and environment collison
(clipping through walls and NPCs), leaving them unable to attack or
defend until killed by zombies.

# WORKAROUNDS

## lockpicking

interacting with and picking a lock and sometimes the map resits the
camera state and restores player control

## Explosives

Throwing a grenade (if controls respond) occasionally braks tghe frozen
camera loop

# ADDITONAL NOTE

This bug ocurs frequently across multiple maps during random encounters
and key story events (eg.ruby diary,time travel,alien encounter).
